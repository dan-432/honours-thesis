\subsection{Aims}

The main aims of this project are:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Compile a list of candidate RNA motifs with potential regulatory or functional roles.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Multiple sequence alignments of 5' UTRs of mRNAs of specific gene families across multiple bacterial species.
        \item Prediction of conserved RNA secondary structures in these alignments.
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item Create a reusable computational pipeline to systematically screen for these motifs.

\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Hypothesis}
Evolutionarily conserved motifs in 5' UTRs of mRNAs of specific gene families will yield structural regulatory motifs.
